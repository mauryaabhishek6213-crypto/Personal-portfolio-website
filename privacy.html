2. Data Collection and Tracking Principles

This portfolio operates under strict data minimization standards:

  • No Advertising or Marketing Trackers: We do not embed ad networks, tracking pixels, or cross-domain user profiling scripts.
  • No Public Visitor Accounts: The website is entirely accessible to public visitors without requiring an account, sign-up, or submission of personal identifying credentials.
  • Local Preference Storage: When you toggle between Light and Dark themes, your choice ("light" or "dark") is saved exclusively in your browser's local storage (key: theme). This data remains entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.
  • Security Diagnostics & Rate Limiting: The web server inspects connection IP addresses solely for rate limiting (maximum 5 failed admin login attempts per 15 minutes) to protect against brute-force attacks. These counters are stored in volatile server memory and expire automatically.
